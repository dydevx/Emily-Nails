## Product Purpose

Existing German-language website for Emily Nails in Goch. Visitors explore nail care, designs, prices and contact the salon by phone or SMS. The user requested a complete layout upgrade and confirmed an elegant, gentle direction retaining pink.

## Users

Inferred from the existing German copy: local salon customers browsing on desktop and mobile before arranging an appointment.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript; preserve section anchors and primary navigation labels. Preserve every published price, business address, phone number, opening time, social profile and copyright text. Booking uses the existing phone/SMS links; there is no online booking backend.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the Emily Nails name and original logo.jpg. User-confirmed style: elegant, gentle, pink. German remains the website language.

## Evidence on Hand

index.html contains business information and pricing. logo.jpg is the original brand mark; 1.jpg is a pedicure photo, menu.jpg is the existing printed price list, and 2.mp4 is the salon's existing video. Do not present generated imagery as actual salon work.
