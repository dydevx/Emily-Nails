# Magazine redesign

Mode: Persuade. User confirmed a whole-page redesign in a beauty-magazine direction. Retain the existing logo palette, German content, local imagery, business facts, navigation and phone/SMS booking.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A beauty magazine with genuinely different compositions as the reader moves through the page. No repeated uniform photo cards or alternating image/text blocks.

OWN-WORLD: Existing ivory, cream, bronze and blush. Self-hosted Cormorant and DM Sans preserve the brand. Large serif headlines, square photographic frames, thin rules, restrained rectangular controls.

STORY: See the nail-care invitation, explore six services, meet the studio, compare the complete prices, browse design inspiration, then call or text.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A two-line 96px maximum headline overlaps the upper blank part of a large right-aligned portrait. Description and appointment action sit below the headline on the left. Practical contact information follows the cover.

FORM: User-selected beauty magazine, code-led. The user's pinned composition overrides the dealt seed 5fcc18e4. Signature interaction is selecting service names to change a shared photographic preview; no automatic slideshow. About is a centered feature story, prices a two-column open menu, gallery an unequal staggered composition, contact a practical closing spread.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
